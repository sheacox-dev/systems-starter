Mockup F: Busy days first. Flow: open the app → see the next day where school and work collide → open another Busy day, or scroll to the quieter days. Filled dots are school, open dots are work or internship, and numbers 1 to 3 mean "do first".

This assumes collisions between school and work are what hurt the student most, so one collision matters more than a full list when they glance at their phone. It also assumes they will scroll past Busy days to find ordinary commitments, such as a shift that starts in 50 minutes.
