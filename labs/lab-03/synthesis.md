# Interview synthesis

Source: `practice-interview.md`. Quotes are copied exactly, including spelling and capitalization.

## 1 and 2. Problems, quotes, and evidence level

Levels: **said** = opinion, feeling, or general claim. **Did** = a past action or event the person
described. **Paid** = money spent.

| # | Problem | Exact quote | Level |
|---|---------|-------------|-------|
| 1 | Employers do not respond after an application | "i received an automated confirmation email, but i never heard anything else. i applied to several other positions and experienced the same thing" | Did |
| | How often problem 1 happens | "it happens pretty frequently i apply to multiple jobs every week, and most employers never respond." | Said |
| 2 | Not knowing the reason for not getting hired | "the hardest part is not knowing why im not getting hired." | Said |
| 3 | Without feedback, it is hard to improve | "not receiving feedback makes it difficult to improve" | Said |
| 4 | The things tried so far have not solved the lack of responses | "i've tried applying to as many jobs as possible, changing my resume slightly for different positions, and following up with employers. i've also tried using different job search websites, but i still struggle to get responses." | Did |

Nothing in the notes reaches **paid**.

The opening line, "gets frustrated not hearing back", describes the friend from the interviewer's
side. It is not one of the friend's answers, so it is not counted as evidence.

## 3. Job-to-be-done

When I'm **applying to multiple jobs every week and most employers never respond**, I want to
**know why I'm not getting hired**, so I can **improve**.

## 5. Three questions I should have asked

1. Walk me through the last application you submitted. What did you do at each step, and how long
   did it take?
2. Tell me about the last time you followed up with an employer. What did you do, and what happened?
3. Have you spent any money on your job search? If so, on what, and what happened?
