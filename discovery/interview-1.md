Interview 1
Role: Coffee Shop Manager 
Date November 22 2026
Notes 
1. Q: Tell me about the last time you had to calculate or distribute tips. What happened, step by step?
A: "At the end of the shift, we collect the cash tips and look at the credit card tips through the POS system. We keep track of how many hours each employee worked and use that information to determine how much everyone receives."
2. Q: What was the hardest part about managing tips?
A: "The hardest part is making sure tips are split fairly. Everyone works different hours and shifts, so we have to make sure the calculations are accurate."
3. Q: How often does this happen?
A: "We collect tips every shift, but we calculate and distribute them weekly or according to our regular pay schedule."
4. Q: What do you do about it now? What tools, spreadsheets, or people are involved?
A: "We use our POS system to track credit card tips. Cash tips are counted separately. We also look at employee schedules and hours worked to calculate everyone's share."
5. Q: What have you tried that did not work?
A: "We've tried keeping track of everything manually, but it can get confusing. Sometimes we have to go back and double-check numbers, especially when employees switch shifts."
