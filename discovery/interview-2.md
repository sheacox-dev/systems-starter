Interview 2
Role: College Student 
Date: November 23 2026
Notes
1. Q: Tell me about the last time you felt overwhelmed trying to balance school, work, and other responsibilities. What happened, step by step?
A: "Last week I had multiple assignments due, shifts at work, and internship responsibilities. I was trying to keep track of everything through different calendars and reminders. I ended up forgetting about an assignment because I was focused on finishing something for work. I had to rush to complete it before the deadline."
2. Q: What was the hardest part?
A: "The hardest part is keeping everything organized when I have so many different responsibilities. Sometimes I don't know what I should prioritize, especially when assignments and work deadlines overlap."
3. Q: How often does this happen?
A: "Pretty much every week. Some weeks are easier than others, but I usually have multiple assignments, work shifts, and internship tasks happening at the same time."
4. Q: What do you do about it now? What tools, spreadsheets, or people are involved?
A: "I use Canvas to check assignments, Google Calendar for my work schedule and personal events, and sometimes the Notes app to write down things I need to get done. I also use reminders on my phone, but everything is spread out across different apps."
5. Q: What have you tried that did not work?
A: "I've tried using a planner and writing down everything I need to do, but I don't always remember to update it. I've also tried making daily to-do lists, but sometimes I add too many things and get overwhelmed when I can't finish everything."
